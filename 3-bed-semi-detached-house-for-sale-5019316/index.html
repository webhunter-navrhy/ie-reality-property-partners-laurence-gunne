Description

A spacious 3 bedroom semi detached family home boasting many extras and ideally located in a well established residential area within walking distance of schools, shops, pharmacies, restaurants and within easy access of Dundalk town centre and the M1 Motorway.

Features include:- on-site parking, attractive rear yard, rockery area, elite fencing front and rear.

The accommodation offers Entrance Porch, Entrance Hall, Spacious living room, fully fitted kitchen and utility area. Overhead there are 3 bedrooms and family bathroom. The main bedroom has an en-suite.

The sale of No., 32 Castle Heights, offers an excellent opportunity to acquire an attractive family home in a convenient and established location.
